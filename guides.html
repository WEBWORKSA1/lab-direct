How we write guides

Every guide is built from primary public sources — MedlinePlus at the US National Library of Medicine, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, the US Preventive Services Task Force, the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements and professional society guidelines — and links to them. We avoid product claims, explain uncertainty where it exists and update guides when guidance changes. Read our editorial policy.

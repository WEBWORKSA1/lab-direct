Advertising standards

  • Sponsored placements are clearly labeled and never change test explanations, ranges or rankings.
  • Laboratory partners must use CLIA-certified labs. Telehealth partners must use licensed clinicians.
  • No unproven diagnostic claims, cure claims or tests marketed without required authorization.
  • We may decline any advertiser at our discretion.

Before you go

  • Bring a photo ID and your printed or emailed lab order (requisition).
  • Check whether the location takes walk-ins or needs an appointment; mornings are busiest.
  • Follow fasting instructions for tests that need them.
  • Drink water unless told otherwise — it makes the draw easier.
  • Ask how results are delivered and how long they take.

Lab networks vs. independent labs

Large national networks run thousands of patient service centers across the US, and most direct-access providers route their orders to one of them. Hospital outpatient labs and independent labs may also accept direct-pay orders. Always confirm the lab accepts the order source you're using.

Our promises to supporters

Support never buys editorial influence. Supporters, sponsors and advertisers can't change what a test page says, and sponsored content is always labeled. We publish fund totals and the allocation above and update them as contributions arrive.

Lab.Direct is an independent publisher, not a registered charity. Contributions support ongoing operations and are not tax-deductible.

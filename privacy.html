Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Lab.Direct ("we", "us") collects when you use lab.direct, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form — a testing-plan request, price quote, decoder summary, newsletter signup, contest entry, donation pledge, job application, lab listing or contact message — we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, ZIP or postal code and your answers. We use it to respond, send what you asked for, run contests and improve the service. Forms are delivered to our inbox by a form-processing service (FormSubmit), which handles the data to deliver it.

Health information: values you type into the results decoder stay in your browser and are not sent to us unless you choose to email yourself a summary. Please don't include sensitive health details in free-text fields. Lab.Direct is not a healthcare provider, and information you send us is not a medical record.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. If you ask for lower-cost options or a testing plan and agree to be contacted, we may share the details needed to fulfil that request with a vetted lab or provider partner. We may share information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Cookies and advertising

We use a small amount of browser storage to remember preferences such as your cookie choice. We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Choosing "Essential only" in our cookie notice requests non-personalized ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message that meets Google's requirements before personalized ads are served.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may collect data under Google's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them, generally no longer than 24 months, unless the law requires longer. We use HTTPS throughout. No method of transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California's CCPA/CPRA or Canada's PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, use our contact form. We'll respond within the time the law requires.

Children

Lab.Direct is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16. Contests are open only to adults.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the effective date. Significant changes will be highlighted on the home page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact us or email us.

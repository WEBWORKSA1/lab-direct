How to use this library

Start with your goal, not a test name. If you feel tired, the usual starting point is a complete blood count, ferritin, thyroid-stimulating hormone, vitamin D and B12. For heart risk, start with a lipid panel and consider ApoB and lipoprotein(a). For blood sugar, an A1c plus a fasting glucose covers most screening. Our testing-plan builder does this matching for you.

About the prices

Prices are estimated typical direct-pay ranges compiled from published prices at online direct-access providers, rounded to whole dollars. They exclude insurance and may exclude a physician-order or draw fee of about $5–$15. Use the price estimator to total a panel.

About the cap colors

Labs collect blood into tubes with different additives, color-coded by cap. The cap shown beside each test is the tube most often used for it. Your lab may use a different tube for the same test; that's normal and doesn't change the result.

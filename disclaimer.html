Medical disclaimer

Lab.Direct publishes general educational information about laboratory tests. Nothing on this site is medical advice, a diagnosis or a recommendation to start, stop or change any treatment. Reference ranges shown are typical adult values from public sources; the range printed on your own lab report takes precedence, and results must be interpreted with your history by a licensed clinician. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number immediately.

Direct-access testing is not available in every state or country, and some tests are not appropriate without clinical evaluation. Lab.Direct is not a laboratory, does not collect or process specimens and does not order tests.

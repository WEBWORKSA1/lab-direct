How we work

Accuracy first: every claim links to a public source and every health page gets a qualified review before it ships. We write for the reader who's anxious about a result at 11 p.m., not for search engines. We work asynchronously, pay on time and credit contributors by name.

Lab.Direct is an equal-opportunity organization. We welcome applicants of every background and make hiring decisions on skills and work samples.

Why cash prices vary so much

The same cholesterol test can cost $10 through one online provider and several times that when billed through a hospital outpatient lab. Direct-access providers negotiate wholesale rates with national lab networks and sell at a fixed cash price, often with a small physician-order fee. Hospitals bill from a chargemaster, and what you pay depends on your insurance, your deductible and whether the lab is in network.

Five ways to pay less

  1. Ask for the cash price first. If you're uninsured or choose not to use insurance, US providers must give you a Good Faith Estimate on request.
  2. Buy panels, not singles. A comprehensive metabolic panel includes 14 tests and usually costs less than ordering several of them separately.
  3. Use pre-tax money. HSA and FSA funds can generally be used for lab tests.
  4. Skip tests you don't need. Large "wellness" bundles often include markers with little evidence behind them.
  5. Watch extra fees. Look for physician-order, draw and kit-shipping fees before checking out.

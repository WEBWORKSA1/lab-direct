Common questions

Can I order a blood test without seeing a doctor?

In most US states, yes. Direct-access providers have an independent physician review and sign the order, then you visit a patient service center or use an at-home kit. A few states restrict direct-access testing, so availability depends on where you live. Read how direct-access testing works.

Does Lab.Direct sell tests?

No. Lab.Direct is an independent guide. We explain tests, estimate typical cash prices and help you find labs and lower-cost options. When we earn money from a partner, we say so on the page.

Are the reference ranges here the same as my lab's?

Not always. Ranges differ between labs, testing methods, ages and pregnancy. The range printed on your own report takes precedence; our decoder uses widely published typical adult ranges for education.

Do I need to fast?

Only for some tests, such as fasting glucose, fasting insulin and iron studies. Most cholesterol screening can be done without fasting. Each test page lists the preparation, and our preparation guide covers the details.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Lab tests for medical care are generally eligible expenses for HSAs and FSAs. Many direct-access providers accept HSA and FSA cards; check with your plan administrator.
